# Privacy Policy - Expenso

*Last Updated: May 1, 2025*

## Introduction

Welcome to Expenso, your personal offline expense tracking application. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to your privacy and explains how your data is handled within the app.

## Our Core Privacy Principles

1. **100% Offline Operation**
   - Expenso operates entirely offline
   - No internet connection is required
   - No data leaves your device

2. **Zero Data Collection**
   - We do not collect any personal information
   - We do not track app usage
   - We do not use analytics
   - We do not have user accounts

3. **Local Storage Only**
   - All expense data stays on your device
   - PDF reports are generated locally
   - Settings are stored on your device only
   - Theme preferences remain local

## App Permissions

Expenso requires minimal permissions:
- **Storage Permission**: Required for
  - Saving expense records
  - Generating PDF reports
  - Storing app settings
  - Managing theme preferences

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Data
- Expense records
- Custom categories
- PDF reports
- App settings
- Theme preferences (Light/Dark mode)

### Security Measures
- All data is stored in secure device storage
- No external server connections
- No cloud backups
- No automatic data transmission

## Data Export and Sharing

When you choose to export or share data:
- PDF reports are generated on-device
- Sharing is handled through your device's system share sheet
- You maintain full control over exported data
- No automatic sharing or uploading

## Your Data Control

You have complete control over your data:
- Add/Edit/Delete expenses at any time
- Generate PDF reports on demand
- Export data when you choose
- Delete all data by uninstalling the app

## No Third-Party Access

We ensure:
- No data sharing with third parties
- No advertising networks
- No analytics services
- No external API calls
- No cloud services integration

## Data Backup

- Backups are your responsibility
- No automatic cloud backup provided
- You can export data manually
- Uninstalling the app removes all data

## Children's Privacy

Expenso does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age.

## App Updates

Updates to Expenso:
- Are provided through GitHub
- Never affect your stored data
- Don't change privacy settings
- Maintain offline-only operation

## Changes to Privacy Policy

Any changes to this policy:
- Will be posted on GitHub
- Won't affect the app's offline nature
- Won't introduce data collection
- Will maintain our privacy-first approach
